# User Manual

## 1. Getting Started
To log in, navigate to the application URL, input your email and password, and click Log In.

## 2. Navigating the Dashboard
The dashboard displays overall metrics (Active Indent Sheets, Process Cost Sheets, Material stock alerts, Cost Variance analytics, and Executive Notification feeds).

## 3. Creating a Business Transaction (Indent Sheet & Process Cost Sheet)
1. Select **Design Module** on the sidebar.
2. Click **Create New Business Transaction**.
3. Fill out product details, customer details, material requirements, and upload drawings/CAD files for the **Indent Sheet**.
4. Define manufacturing processes (Turning, Heat Treatment, Grinding, Assembly, Inspection), vendor/in-house selections, and planned costs for the **Process Cost Sheet**.
5. Click **Submit Business Transaction** (Advances state to `Design Completed` and sends automated notifications to Stores, Senior Manager, and General Manager).
